Tech Muscle Quiz

Building the tech muscle of your business leaders. Read the key ideas, then do five reps: one question each, with feedback after every answer.

Key ideas

Idea 1

Leaders can't hand tech off to IT

Tech-capable leaders own the transformation of their business domain. They have four capabilities:

  • Craft visions with the customer at the center
  • Build tech-enabled roadmaps with use cases and KPIs
  • Oversee tech delivery, working iteratively
  • Lead end-to-end change management
Idea 2

Tech muscle is built through habits

  • Learn like it's your mission
  • Fixate on value, not quick proofs of concept
  • It's never just about the tech
  • Get your hands dirty
  • Develop a nose for tech talent
  • Get comfortable with ambiguity and stage-gate your efforts
Idea 3

The C-suite has to build the bench

Most large companies run 15–30 core processes, so they need about 75–150 tech-capable leaders. Three actions:

  • Put the right people and incentives in place
  • Launch a strategic upskilling program
  • Fix the operating model, e.g. persistent funding
Company example

Citizens Bank: Adam Boyd

Adam set out to reinvent home equity lending: pre-approved credit lines, a few-click application and an automated back office, so customers get money in days instead of the industry's 2–6 weeks. He led four cross-functional agile teams, learned the bank's tech stack, owned the change management, and spends 6–10 hours a week learning about tech.
